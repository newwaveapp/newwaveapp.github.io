Privacy Policy — Hangul Mission: Learn Korean

Last updated: 2026-02-26

This Privacy Policy explains how Hangul Mission: Learn Korean (“the App”) collects, uses, and shares information.

1) Summary

  • The App does not require account registration.
  • The App is designed to work offline.
  • The App stores learning progress locally on your device.

2) Information We Collect

A. Data stored on your device (local only)

The App saves learning-related settings and progress on your device, such as:

  • Selected difficulty setting
  • Daily goal and streak status
  • Pack progress (best score, completed count, resume position)

This data is stored locally using Android’s app storage (e.g., SharedPreferences). It is not automatically transmitted to us.

B. Data we do not collect

We do not collect:

  • Your name, email address, phone number
  • Precise location
  • Contacts, photos, or files
  • Payment information

3) How We Use Information

Local data is used only to:

  • Remember your settings and progress
  • Show daily goals and streaks
  • Resume missions and display scores

4) Sharing of Information

We do not sell or share personal information with third parties. Because the App does not transmit your local progress to our servers, there is no server-side sharing.

5) Third-Party Services

The App may use built-in Android services such as Text-to-Speech (TTS) to speak words and syllables. Any processing performed by the device’s TTS engine is governed by the policies of the device/OS provider.

If the App adds third-party SDKs in the future (e.g., analytics or ads), this policy will be updated.

6) Data Retention & Deletion

Progress and settings remain on your device until you:

  • Clear the App’s storage, or
  • Uninstall the App

7) Children’s Privacy

The App is intended for general audiences. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

8) Security

We take reasonable measures to keep the App secure. However, no method of storage is 100% secure.

9) Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. We will update the “Last updated” date accordingly.
