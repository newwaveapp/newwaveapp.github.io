We may collect information directly or through third-party SDKs used in the App. The types of information that may be collected include:

The App may store certain information locally on your device, such as:

The App uses Google Play Games Services to support leaderboard features. If you choose to sign in with Google Play Games, your player identity and gameplay-related data, such as submitted scores, may be processed to provide leaderboard and related game services.
